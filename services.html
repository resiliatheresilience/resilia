COUNSELLING & PSYCHOTHERAPY

Therapeutic Services

Resilia offers psychological counselling and psychotherapy for individuals, children, adolescents, couples and families. Therapeutic work is tailored to the individual, drawing on evidence-based frameworks to support emotional wellbeing, self-understanding and meaningful change.

01
Individual Counselling

Individual Counselling

For adults navigating personal, emotional and life-related concerns. Sessions provide a space to explore thoughts, feelings and experiences with support and professional guidance.

02
Adolescent Counselling

Adolescent Counselling

For teenagers navigating emotional wellbeing, stress, relationships, identity and developmental concerns. Sessions are adapted to the adolescent's pace and comfort.

03
Child Counselling

Child Counselling

For children experiencing emotional, behavioural, school-related or adjustment concerns. Therapeutic approaches are age-appropriate, creative and child-centred.

04
Anxiety Support

Support for Anxiety

Support to understand anxiety, notice patterns and develop practical ways to respond to worry and its impact on daily life.

05
Depression Support

Support for Depression

A supportive space to explore low mood, loss of interest and related challenges, and work toward manageable steps at an individual pace.

06
Stress Management

Stress Management

Practical psychological support to recognize stressors, build coping strategies and restore balance across work, study and personal life.

07
Couples Counselling

Couples Counselling

For couples navigating communication difficulties, relationship concerns or significant life transitions. Sessions provide a neutral, supportive space for reflection and dialogue.

08
Family Counselling

Family Counselling

For families working through interpersonal dynamics, communication patterns or transitions that affect the family system.

09
Grief & Loss

Grief & Loss

For individuals processing experiences of grief, loss or significant life change. Sessions provide a space to navigate these experiences with care and psychological support.

How It Works
