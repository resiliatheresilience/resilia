WHY RESILIA

People are more than the challenges they are going through.

Resilia is a psychology and wellbeing practice built around a simple belief — people are more than the challenges they are going through.

Our work focuses on creating a warm, empathetic and non-judgmental space where individuals can understand themselves, make sense of their experiences and develop ways of navigating life's challenges with greater awareness and resilience.

Resilia brings together psychological support, counselling and assessment with an emphasis on empathy, individuality and meaningful human connection.

HOW IT WORKS

A space without judgement.

Psychological support can look different for everyone.

At Resilia, sessions are approached with:
Listen → Understand → Explore → Work Together → Grow

Rather than simply focusing on a problem, the aim is to understand the person, their experiences and the context surrounding their concerns.
