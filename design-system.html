RESILIA Design System

“Journey of nurturing Resilience”

Compassionate Support. Evidence-Based Care. Lasting Change.

The visual identity for Resilia establishes a calm, safe, human, and sophisticated presence. It communicates trust and emotional safety without resorting to sterile hospital tropes, medical cliches, or generic templates.

02 / Identity Assets

Official Resilia Logo Marks

The Resilia brand mark integrates the classical Greek letter Ψ (Psi)—universal emblem of the psyche—blooming like a botanical lotus within the sweeping grace of an architectural R.
