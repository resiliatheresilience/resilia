For Professional Referrals

Resilia welcomes referrals from GPs, paediatricians, psychiatrists, educators, social workers and other health and allied health professionals.

TRUSTED REFERRAL PARTNER

A trusted referral partner.

Resilia offers psychological counselling and assessment services for individuals, children, adolescents, couples and families. We work collaboratively with referring professionals to ensure that care is coordinated, well-communicated and in the best interest of the individual.

Referrals are welcomed from GPs, paediatricians, psychiatrists, educators, school counsellors, social workers and other health and allied health professionals.

Following an initial consultation, a summary letter can be provided to the referring professional with the individual's consent.

REFERRAL INFORMATION

What to Include

To help us prepare for an initial consultation, the following information is helpful — though not always required.

  • The individual's name, age and contact details
  • A brief summary of the presenting concern or reason for referral
  • Relevant background — medical, developmental, educational or psychological history
  • Any previous assessments or reports (where available and with consent)
  • Preferred mode of contact for the referring professional

All information shared is handled in accordance with professional confidentiality standards.

AVAILABLE SERVICES

Services Available for Referral

Individual Counselling

For adults navigating personal, emotional and life-related concerns.

Adolescent & Child Counselling

For children and teenagers experiencing emotional, behavioural or developmental concerns.

Couples & Family Counselling

For couples and families working through relational, communication or systemic concerns.

Psychological Assessments

Cognitive, learning, emotional, behavioural and developmental assessments. Assessment findings are shared via a feedback session and, where appropriate, a written report.
