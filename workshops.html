WORKSHOPS & PSYCHOEDUCATION

Workshops &
Psychoeducation

Resilia aims to create accessible psychological education around topics relevant to everyday emotional wellbeing.

Bringing psychology into everyday life.

Workshops and psychoeducation sessions at Resilia are designed to build psychological literacy — helping individuals, families and organisations better understand mental health, emotional wellbeing and the tools that support a more resilient life.

Sessions are facilitated by Rutuja Aughade, Psychologist (Clinical & Counselling) | Facilitator — and are tailored for the audience, setting and purpose.

AREAS OF FOCUS

Workshop Topics

Resilia workshops cover a range of themes relevant to psychological wellbeing, emotional health and resilience.

Stress & Anxiety Management Emotional Regulation Mindfulness & Psychological Wellbeing Grief & Loss Resilience Building Adolescent Mental Health Parenting & Child Development Relationships & Communication Self-Compassion & Identity Workplace Wellbeing

Topics are illustrative. Workshop content is tailored to the group and context. Enquire for bespoke sessions.
