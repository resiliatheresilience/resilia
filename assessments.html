PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENTS

Psychological Assessments

Psychological assessments can help build a clearer understanding of cognitive, emotional, behavioural and individual functioning.

Start Your Conversation Submit an Enquiry

A structured and sensitive approach to understanding.

Assessments at Resilia are detailed, structured and sensitively conducted. They provide an evidence-based way of understanding areas such as cognitive functioning, learning, emotional regulation, behaviour and development.

Assessment findings are shared in a clear, accessible way — and where appropriate, a written report is provided to support next steps.

ASSESSMENT AREAS

Assessment Services

Resilia offers assessments across the following areas. All assessments are conducted by Rutuja Aughade, Psychologist (Clinical & Counselling) | Facilitator.

01

Cognitive & Intellectual Assessment

Assessments to understand cognitive functioning, intellectual abilities and areas of strength and difficulty. Relevant for educational planning, learning support and developmental understanding.

02

Learning Disability Assessment

Assessments to explore the presence of specific learning differences that may be affecting academic performance, literacy, numeracy or information processing.

03

Emotional & Behavioural Assessment

Assessments to understand emotional regulation, behavioural patterns and psychological functioning — relevant for children, adolescents and adults experiencing significant emotional or behavioural difficulties.

04

Developmental Assessment

Assessments to support understanding of developmental milestones, social communication and adaptive functioning. Conducted with care and sensitivity for children and adolescents.

05

Career & Vocational Guidance

Assessments to support individuals in understanding their strengths, interests and abilities in relation to career planning, academic direction or vocational choices.

06

Personality Assessment

A structured exploration of personality traits, preferences and patterns to support self-understanding and guide appropriate recommendations.

07

Screening Assessment

Screening tools can help identify areas that may benefit from further assessment or support. Screening is an initial step and is not a diagnosis.

08

Clinical Assessment

A clinically guided assessment of presenting concerns, history and functioning to help clarify needs and inform appropriate next steps.

THE PROCESS

What to Expect

01

Initial Consultation

A conversation to understand what you are looking for and to determine which assessment approach is most relevant.

02

Assessment Sessions

Detailed, structured sessions conducted in a calm and supportive environment. The number of sessions will depend on the assessment type.

03

Scoring & Analysis

Results are carefully analysed and interpreted in the context of the individual's background, history and presenting concerns.

04

Feedback & Report

A feedback session to share findings clearly and, where appropriate, a written report to support next steps — educational, clinical or vocational.

An Important Note

Assessments at Resilia do not include diagnostic conclusions or clinical diagnoses unless conducted within a multi-disciplinary clinical framework. Assessment findings are shared to support understanding, planning and onward referral where appropriate.
